Zsosa's Timepiece

Roll to Hit
Roll Damage
Momentary Stasis
Mark 1 Stress to make a spellcasting role against a creature, the targeted creature must make an Instinct roll. If the Instinct roll is lower than the caster's spellcasting roll, the targeted creature is Immobilized until the creature's spotlight returns or the GM spends a Fear to remove it.
Temporal Awareness
Spend 1 Hope, start your spotlight phase immediately. If another player is currently in the spotlight, describe how you work with them to take your turn.
Flicker In Time
Spend 2 Hope to immediately clear 1 stress from or give 1 Hope to up to 5 allies.
A polished brass pocket watch embellished with intricate line work depicting an overlapping sun and crescent moon. Hidden amongst the design are the initials of its creator “ACZ.” Inside, the clock face is in common numerals listed clockwise, one through twelve, there is also a secondary small face that seems to track the phases of the moon. Zsosa's Timepiece has three different phases of operation that correspond to its user's power level, as well as their understanding of chronomancy and the timepiece itself. You instinctively always know exactly what time it is.

Occasionally, the ticking of the inner mechanisms of the timepiece drown out all other sounds in your mind for a short time, even while deafened.
Distort Time:
You can create a 15-foot-radius sphere of temporal distortion, centered on yourself, that allows you and any friendly creature that enters the space to peer into the past and comb through the events of that space, up to 24 hours ago.

This effect lasts 10 minutes, or until dismissed.
Timeless Body:
Once fully empowered, for every 10 years that pass your body only ages 1 year. This effect ends if you no longer control the pocket watch.

Staff of Relentless Storms

Roll to Hit
Roll Damage
Thunderstrike
Once per short rest, slam the staff against the ground to create a deafening shock wave emanating from you. All adversaries must make an Instinct roll of 18 or are Vulnerable until you take your next turn or the GM spends 1 Fear to end the effect early.
Per short rest
Call Lightning
Spend 1 Hope. Conjure a beam of lightning in the direction you are facing that reaches Very Far range. Any adversary within this area must succeed an Agility roll of 18 or take 5d6 lightning damage.
Unending Torrent
Spend 1 Hope to summon a powerful stream of water directed towards an enemy or enemies of your choosing in a line. Adversaries must make a Strength roll of 16 or be pushed into the next furthest range from you. This effect can also be used to affect the environment around you, including putting out non-magical fires.

Crackling Infusion: Spend 1 Hope to add 2d6 to your next damage roll.
A gnarled wooden staff that seems to pulse down the weapon with a blue, crackling energy that disperses into the ground. Though it doesn't have a connection to the blue dragonflight, it's similarity to the trident used to control Elliyah does not go unnoticed.

Diadem of Brilliance

Untouched by Flame
Spend 2 Hope, become immune to fire damage until the end of the scene. Removing the diadem also removes this effect.
Dazzling Light
Spend 1 Hope, create a sphere of light that reaches to Far range until the end of scene. While within this dome of light, you are unaffected by magical darkness.
Starfall
Conjure spectral daggers made of starlight that circle your head. Spend 1 Hope to deal 6d10+8 damage to a creature within Far range.
Forceful Hold
Spend 2 Hope, create a force field around a creature that you can see until your next spotlight, or until the GM spends 1 Fear to dispel the effect.
Originally forged by the Order of the Orachalcum for Jasper. This diadem was meant to keep Jasper satiated and safe while working amongst the Brass dragonflight. As Jasper aged, the diadem's purpose expanded to fit his new role as “The Golden Child”. Infused directly with the magic of the Brass' breath, the once humble object of defense became Jasper's bag of tricks to turn the tide of any fight they faced.
