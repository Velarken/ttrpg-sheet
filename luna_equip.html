    Starpiercer
    Originally created by the goddess Sehanine Moonbow to combat the Titans and the forces of Tiamat the Dragon Queen, The Star-Piercer Bow is both a holy artifact and powerful weapon. The bow itself is made of the incredibly rare material known only as “Star Metal” and strung with magically enhanced Silverweave thread. Star-Piercer was gifted to the Dwarven Hero Nee'indra Brightbow during the first dragon crisis, and with it she hunted the white dragonflight to near extinction and helped establish the Dwarvern Kingdom of Rusond.

    Star-Piercer became forever intertwined with the bloodline of Nee'indra Brightbow after being cursed and shattered by the fae witch Glorsharna, from that day forward, the first born women of each generation became doomed to find glory but never be recognized or remembered by history or those around them. Glorsharna kept one piece of the bow and Nee'indra passed the remaining three pieces on to her children as a reminder of what was lost. Over time however, the legend was forgotten until Star-Piercer was reassembled a by direct descendant of Nee'indra, Luna Hyguard, breaking the curse.
    Legacy of the Hyguards
    • Star-Piercer requires no ammo, instead firing arrows of conjured force energy.
    • You cannot be charmed or frightened, especially by dragons.
    • You can magically summon or dismiss Star-piercer at will.
    • Gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls while using this weapon
    Weapon Abilities
    Lunar Wrath
    Choose an elemental damage type to use for your arrows. Spend 1 Hope to add a second damage type to your next attack.
    Dark Side of the Moon
    Spend 2 Hope Until end of scene, deal an additional 2d8 per attack. Additionally, you gain advantage on all attacks made with Starpiercer
    Volley of Stars
    Spend 2 Hope. Roll to hit up to 5 targets, on a success inflict a Minor wound. If you choose to target 1 enemy, roll 5 dice. On 3 or more successes, inflict a Severe wound and make the target Vulnerable.
    Burning Starlight
    Spend 3 Hope to ensure your next attack is a critical success.
    Blue Scale Armor
    This armor was crafted from the remains of an adult blue dragon plaguing the city of Gin, the mighty Boujie bunch felled Zarian on the streets of the city. Using the bone and scales from the dragons corpse the Boujie Bunch commissioned specialty armor from Ishida Armor Works. Tsuna and Hiromi forged a matching set of armor to protect the wearer from future encounters with blue dragons.
    Armor Abilities
    - While wearing this armor, gain +1 Evasion.
    - You also have advantage on rolls to maintain your composure in the face of a dragon's terror. Additionally, gain advantage against the breath weapon of dragons.
    - Gain resistance to lightning damage
    Predator To Prey
    Spend 1 Hope, you are able to magically discern the distance and direction to the nearest black dragon. You are able to track multiple dragons using this sense, but their exact positions may be less accurate.
    Cloak of Swift Flight
    A shimmering, translucent capelet that looks almost too delicate to be worn. Though it looks fragile, this cape is able to allow its user to evade attacks and even fly.
    Item Abilities
    Mach Speed
    Spend 2 Hope. You gain +2 to your Evasion score until the end of the scene.
    Above It All
    Once per rest, take flight. You are able to use any of your domain cards or equipment while flying.

    While flying, if shooting with Starpiercer, spend 1 Hope to add 2d8 to your next arrow.
    Gauntlets of Ogre Power
    While wearing these gauntlets, your Strength score is 4.
