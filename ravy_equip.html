Shadowmere
Weapon Lore
Shadowmere is a semi-sentient sword made of onyx and Star metal, serving as an extension of Mask, the God of Thieves. Shadowmere is somewhat chaotic in nature and may occasionally speak to its wielder and encourage them to get into mischief or even steal any valuables it may sense in the area. While chaotic neutral in its nature, Shadowmere's purpose also gives it a hunger, a craving for the life force desired by Mask to help him restore his strength, as a result, Shadowmere drinks in the anima of any and all creatures that it slays. This weapon can only be picked up by its wielder, all others attempting to lift the blade will find it extremely heavy and impossible to lift.
Weapon Abilities
God Forged
This weapon is infused with ancient magics, gain a +2 bonus to your attack and damage rolls made with Shadowmere.
Lingering Shadows
Spend 1 Hope to empower your next attack; the wound left by your empowered blade oozes shadow and cannot be healed.
Not So Fast
If an enemy attempts to move out of your range, spend 1 Hope to traverse the shadows and appear behind your target.
Become Shadow
While you are vulnerable or restrained, spend 3 Hope to remove your conditions. You and your belongings melt into the shadows of your surroundings, reappear Hidden withing Close range.
Black Scale Armor
This armor was crafted from the remains from the twin black dragons Bile and Rancor. Using the bone and scales from the dragons corpse the Boujie Bunch commissioned specialty armor from Ishida Armor Works. Tsuna and Hiromi forged a matching set of armor to protect the wearer from future encounters with black dragons.
Armor Abilities
- While wearing this armor, gain +1 Evasion.
- You also have advantage on rolls to maintain your composure in the face of a dragon's terror. Additionally, gain advantage against the breath weapon of dragons.
- Gain resistance to acid damage.
Predator To Prey
Spend 1 Hope, you are able to magically discern the distance and direction to the nearest black dragon. You are able to track multiple dragons using this sense, but their exact positions may be less accurate.
Cloak of Mask's Return
A blood-red cape covered in blinking eyes; while wearing it, you can see in all directions. The eyes on this cloak look two-dimensional, like patches or embroidery, but their blinking can sometimes unsettle others.
Item Abilities
Focused Sight
Spend 1 Hope to gain advantage to your next Instinct roll.
I See It All
Spend 1 Hope to see into the Ethereal Plane, revealing invisible creatures and objects up to Very Far range. Lasts until you rest, or scene ends.
You Can't See Me
Spend 1 Hope to give yourself advantage on Finesse rolls to Hide.
Take Flight
Once per long rest, you gain the ability to fly until the end of the scene. Taking Severe or Massive damage will cancel the effect early, forcing you to land.

Once per long rest:

Runed Gloves of Theivery
A singluar, tight-fitting leather glove with an oddly glowing symbol on the palm of the hand. These model of gloves were created to allow fighters a way to quickly escape combat; however, a subsect of their users find themselves with a sudden knack for kleptomania.
Quick Escape
Once per long rest, use the magic stored in the rune to create a magical smokescreen in the area to Far range. While within the smoke, you are able to see normally, however allies and enemies may not.
Finders Keepers
While surrounded by your smokescreen, gain advantage on all rolls to Hide or steal an item.
